# TRR-0012 operational lesson

The avoidable delays came from environment and path integration defects: a warmed bridge smoke hid a cold-process import failure, and a later record-position shape mismatch reached the scorer boundary. Future work should qualify the cold full entrypoint and scorer together on a synthetic shape fixture before reserving compute. Serial handoffs across preparation, capture, repair, and scoring also added coordination latency and obscured phase ownership. Assign one owner to each complete phase, with a concrete artifact and handoff at its boundary. When a deterministic repair is required, release the GPU immediately and resume only after the bounded repair check passes. Keep create-only outputs, source and tensor hashes, and truth-opening gates intact throughout the repair so operational shortcuts cannot change the scientific record.
