# Final runner and runtime audit

**Reviewer scope:** read-only audit of the final public runner, historical comparator, freeze/evaluation gate, and the timed CPU discrepancy. No final predictions were scored and no private truth was opened during this review.

## Source and access disposition

The audit compares the published baseline at `6b618760f50055dc5c8a62e830ab7a9761190cfe` with the final runner/evaluator at `6e05feeade57593cdadea2d4db4ce40085a51f59`. The later progress-only source update is `e43a595d0f4300d5db8f93c86881b455dfa30ea4`; it adds flushed phase telemetry and does not change numerical settings or prediction logic. The relevant frozen-table/qualifier implementation is covered by `42dc8feac301d690eb292e555641cc73a3d062d0` and the CPU qualification by `9fb635a7f66866da05a08cb6084da7b1704f13a3`.

I found no scientific or access blocker in the final source. `scripts/trr_p01/reconstruct.py` loads only the sanitized public interface, rejects truth/source/dataset/target-model/target-LoRA/condition inputs, and requires the opaque record order, all-valid 40-position geometry, BF16 observations, and finite values. The public prediction rows bind the record ID/order, sequence and prediction values, mask/position/observation digests, model metadata, configuration/evidence/table hashes, and `truth_opened=false`. Evaluator-private target/condition mappings remain outside the attack process.

`scripts/trr_p01/freeze_score.py` validates the public configuration, index, observation tensor, prediction tensor/JSONL one-to-one order and shapes, and all referenced hashes before scoring. In `score_frozen`, this validation occurs before resolving or reading the private truth/manifest. That is the required pre-truth gate; duplicate/foreign IDs, changed observations, wrong metadata or shapes, and prediction tampering should therefore fail closed before truth access. The external evaluator source-certification sidecar records the final committed source and clean-tree verification; the absence of a code-commit field in the native evidence is disclosed by that sidecar and orchestration record.

The historical control preserves the published fixed decision rule: frozen lens policy `a1a2_43ea0bb737bc075531ca`, `torch.topk(..., k=512, sorted=True)` proposal order, first 256 candidates, direct cosine scoring on the public-prefix simulation, and `argmax` in published A1 candidate order. It always commits a candidate and introduces no new tie policy, adaptive threshold, abstention, centering, or K32 variant. The 16-by-40 execution is explicitly a geometry/input-output port of the published control, without training or retraining.

## Runtime discrepancy

The prior static-only evidence is `experiments/TRR-P01/runtime/reconstruct-arm-000-20260905/reconstructor_evidence.json`; its recorded static phase is `11.572758414999043 s` for the 16-record panel. It used the same full-vocabulary table and four static arms as the final plan. A separate post-BOS public lookup of 256 queries took about 1.60 s, and the representative CPU qualifier passed its largest declared K256 cell (8 records, 2048 copied-cache candidates at position 39) with peak RSS about 5.48 GiB and total time about 5.41 s.

The timed final arm had 16 records and 39 scored post-BOS positions, hence **624 actual scored queries per method**, not 640: `16 * (40 - 1)`. The static portion makes 624 queries for each of boundary cosine/L2 and raw-embedding cosine/L2. The old run timed out without flushed phase events, so its manually recorded “still in static full-vocabulary lookup” label is not evidence that static lookup was the active phase.

The most likely bottleneck is reference correction. `apply_reference_correction` is record/position-wise, and the final runner invokes it separately for cosine and L2: up to `16 * 39 * 2 = 1,248` one-query nearest calls. Each call revalidates the full `[128256, 2048]` prototype table and repeats prototype normalization/chunked sorting, creating many small CPU kernels and repeated full-table work. The qualifier measured only one reference probe per record and did not exercise this full correction matrix. Historical A1+A2 evaluation follows correction and is also material: for one 16-record run it entails 78 A1/candidate groups with record batch at most 8, 159,744 candidate simulations/commits, and three A1 proposal forwards for 624 post-BOS queries. Thus the timeout’s active phase remains unknown until the new `phase_progress.jsonl` telemetry is captured; the correction loop is the leading explanation, with historical evaluation a secondary possible contributor. No numerical or batching change is justified by this diagnosis.

For accounting, the correction path has 1,248 reference probes and 1,280 persistent-prefix commits across both metrics when run record-wise. The historical path has 159,744 candidate simulations/commits, 640 persistent commits, 158 public-prefix forward groups, and 160,384 prefix input-token evaluations. These are distinct from static distance comparisons and should be reported separately from wall time and serialization/I/O.

## Freeze and receipt limitations; joint pre-score plan

The runner writes method evidence before predictions and route output, while compute/method timings finish before output serialization. `finish_receipt.json` records later save/hash/I/O timings and artifact hashes, but the current freeze validator does not consume or bind the route/finish receipt. Therefore compute timing and output-I/O timing should remain separate, and any claim that every timing record is cryptographically bound should include the supplemental receipt/sidecar evidence. The receipt is create-only but has no independent external anchor; this is a provenance limitation outside the tested single-artifact tamper gate. The truth file is also validated after the pre-truth gate, but its hash is not bound to the evaluator manifest before opening.

Before opening any private truth, the supplemental joint pre-score freeze should independently freeze and validate both final arm bundles with `freeze_score.py`, verify both receipts/evidence records, artifact hashes, configuration/table/observation hashes, and identical opaque record order/geometry, and confirm complete declared arm coverage. Only after both arm bundles pass that joint pre-score check should the evaluator-private condition mapping be opened and `score_frozen` be called for each condition. This preserves the separation between target construction/evaluation and attack execution and ensures an incomplete or tampered arm cannot be scored silently.

**Disposition:** source/access review passes with the timing and receipt limitations above disclosed. Await progress-telemetry evidence and the final frozen numerical matrix before making a scientific-results judgment.
