# TRR-0007 training-curve interpretation

`fit_learning_curves.png` and `fit_learning_curves_plot_data.json` are descriptive summaries of the four existing public fitting receipts. The renderer is the existing `scripts/trr0007_fit_learning_curve.py`; it reads only the four `learning_curve.json` files listed in the plot-data receipt. The left panel is held-out public-development style-balanced token accuracy. The right panel is the seeded initially-wrong challenge from the fitting bank. Each marker is the receipt's selected step, defined as the earliest maximum of held-out development style-balanced accuracy.

At the selected markers, held-out development accuracy is 0.954711 for the current trained-diagonal fit, 0.956996 for the current residual MLP-512 fit, 0.957837 for the improved trained-diagonal fit, and 0.956848 for the improved residual MLP-512 fit. The improved trained-diagonal cell is the highest of these four by 0.003126 over the current trained-diagonal cell. The improved residual cell is 0.000149 below the current residual cell. These are small descriptive differences from the existing public development curves.

The initially-wrong challenge reaches 0.999512 for the current trained-diagonal fit and 1.0 for the other three cells at their selected markers. This challenge is sampled from fitting-bank positions that the neutral initialization initially misclassified (`2,048` rows from `84,826` current-bank initially-wrong rows or `84,022` improved-bank initially-wrong rows). It tests whether optimization can repair initially wrong public fitting examples; it is not an independent held-out evaluation. Its near-saturation therefore says that all four fits can fit this challenge and leaves little separation between them.

All four selected checkpoints report train token accuracy 1.0, while held-out development accuracy remains about 0.955–0.958. That train-versus-development gap is consistent with successful optimization plus residual generalization or representation error, but these curves cannot identify which cause dominates. The public development curve is the checkpoint-selection signal; train accuracy and the initially-wrong fit challenge are supporting diagnostics.

This figure does not establish a fresh-evaluation gain, a causal advantage for broader support, or a final capacity decision. The current/improved bank and diagonal/residual architecture factors are shown for orientation, while the fresh evaluator remains responsible for its frozen selection, exclusions, and truth-separated scoring.
