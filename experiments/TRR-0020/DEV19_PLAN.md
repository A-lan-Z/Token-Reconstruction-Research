# Development19: read the fitted mixture embedding directly

## Motivation and fixed hypothesis
The completed native objective audit evaluated768frozen canonical reconstructions. In both benchmarks and all three no-shortlist methods, every qualifying first mistake with correct common history had larger native activation error than the baseline's correct token. For the latest refinement these counts are9Pile and98Finance. This demonstrates an optimization/readout gap in those comparisons, not impossibility or global optimality.

The existing Gini optimizer fits a probability-weighted embedding through the prefix, then emits the largest logit. Largest probability and nearest embedding to the fitted mean are different decisions. Test whether reading the optimized mean recovers information discarded by the argmax. A tiny counterexample and independent distance identities are checked on CPU. That example alone says nothing about the actual Llama vocabulary.

## Exact study
Use the unchanged Gini003 optimizer and its frozen constants, with32,64,128or256steps. No alteration to initialization, derivatives, loss, update, reset schedule or numerical geometry. Keep full-vocabulary support throughout. At the last soft state, recompute the exact declared BF16-probability/BF16-weight mixture with FP32accumulation. Compare that mean with EVERY one of128256embeddings using four fixed readouts: raw Euclidean, raw cosine, existing white-metric Euclidean and white-metric cosine. There is no top-k, proposal window or subsequent A2 verification. Prefix weights stay frozen; there are zero offline or online model-parameter fitting steps.

Let D be the FP32 copy of supplied BF16 embeddings, R the existing prefix-derived metric and m the fitted mean. Compute A=mD^T and B=((mR)R^T)D^T. Euclidean scores are2A-||D||^2 and2B-||DR||^2; cosine rankings divide A or B by the respective embedding norms (the query norm is a positive per-position constant and is omitted). Squared embedding norms clamp at1e-24only for the cosine denominator. TF32 is disabled. White norms are built in declared4096-row geometry. No equivalence to a differently batched score is assumed.

Four optimizer configurations x eight previously opened development inputs =32cells, each emitting the four readouts plus unchanged soft snapshots. Save the fitted FP32 mean and final largest probability for later diagnostics. No source truth until all32cells and all variant outputs are frozen, source/input/output bindings pass, and three negative truth-gate cases are rejected. This is retrospective development; no active canonical method is registered yet.
