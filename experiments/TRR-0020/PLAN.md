## New hypothesis
Use a factorization of A2 to score ALL 128256 vocabulary entries at each reconstruction update, without handing a top-K list to another verifier. A current complete sequence estimate supplies the context correction, computed by one ordinary prefix forward pass. For each vocabulary token v, a deterministic prefix-derived descriptor D(v) represents the context-independent computation. Approximate the activation at each position by D(v) + [F(current sequence)_position - D(current token)]. Compare the entire vocabulary under this approximation and emit one revised sequence estimate. This is an approximate A2 decision rule, not exact native A2, and its usefulness must be measured.

Test descriptors: raw embedding; MLP-only intrinsic pass; exact public-prefix BOS+token pass. No training/fitting. Test whitened cosine residual cancellation with fixed residual step 0.5 and 1.0, and raw activation-space shifted cosine for intrinsic and BOS descriptors. Use 32 fixed whole-sequence refinement steps, retain snapshots at 0,1,2,4,8,16,32; keep best complete sequence and best per-position cosine-residual estimate as separately reported diagnostics. Every update scores the full vocabulary. A2 sees only the one current whole-sequence estimate, never a token shortlist.
