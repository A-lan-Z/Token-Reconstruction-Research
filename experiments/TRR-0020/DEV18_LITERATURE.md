# Literature triage during development18

[Recovering Input Text from Hidden States, July2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.00852v1) studies tokenwise continuous optimization on GPT-2 last-layer states. Its algorithm then retrieves nearest embeddings and verifies candidates; reported operating points use100,2000,or10000candidates. Thus its high-accuracy results do not satisfy this task's no-shortlist requirement. It also assumes access to the model that emitted the activation, whereas our reconstruction uses a supplied approximation to an unavailable target prefix. The paper's reported9.73to27.5seconds per token on its hardware/task cannot be treated as a runtime comparison with our cut4Llama benchmarks. The observation that continuous and discrete objectives can disagree motivates diagnostics, but does not establish a new qualified method here. No code, weights or external artifacts were downloaded or executed.

Internal prior-art check: scripts/trr0020/full_vocabulary.py already tests embedding, intrinsicMLP and BOS descriptors with additive residual cancellation; dev1 failed. scripts/trr0014/reverse_probe.py already tests sublayer damped/Anderson inversions. scripts/trr0017/joint_projection.py already tests whole-sequence continuous Adam/LBFGS and periodic projection. These are not new unexplored alternatives.
