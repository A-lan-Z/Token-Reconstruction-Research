# TRR-0009 prospective planning proposal

This proposal tests a bounded readout-adaptation question while preserving the TRR-0008 retained-reference decision. It compares one published competent decoder under three predeclared arms: the unchanged current-bank residual checkpoint, continued supervised fitting with the public embedding readout fixed, and the same continuation with a supported-token post-logit gain and bias. The retained TRR-0006 trained-diagonal state remains visible context and is not refit or used as a new primary pair.

The common starting state is the published TRR-0007 current-bank residual MLP checkpoint at selected step 2100. Its state binding is `experiments/TRR-0007/enriched_fit_v1/current_enriched/trr0007_residual_mlp512/selected.safetensors`, 29,390,628 bytes, SHA-256 `2a44a91b01ca1fdc9615eab804872c50c606199704eaf51fa114cc0d7959ddc8`. The published improved fitting bank has 1,200 records and 124,371 post-BOS positions, with the shared 3,000-step, seed-4005, batch-8 schedule (`5a2daa0087b1877bb5f9be4bd59ef201a4fa6478fcd5b16a1b88808963eab472`). Both continued arms use the same decoder learning rate of 2e-4, 3,000-step cosine schedule, zero weight decay, gradient clipping at 1, and validation selection including step zero at 100-step checkpoints. The adaptable arm adds a scalar readout group at learning rate 1e-3.

The current-bank prefit choice supersedes the earlier improved-bank starting-state proposal because the improved-bank residual had already reached 100% on its own fitting bank. The superseded proposal is retained at `experiments/TRR-0009/training/design_v1_prefit_improved_state_superseded.md` and is not a scientific run.

The adaptable readout is a direct post-logit correction for the 17,126 token IDs supported by the improved fitting bank:

```text
logit_v = base_logit_v * gain_v + bias_v
```

It starts at `gain=1` and `bias=0`, clips the gain and bias magnitude at 0.25, and uses the predeclared frequency-weighted penalty on raw gain and raw bias. The penalty is `1e-4 * mean((1 + 4/sqrt(count_v)) * (raw_gain_v^2 + raw_bias_v^2))` over supported tokens with positive fitting-bank frequency. Absent IDs have no correction parameter, remain at the public readout, and never enter a count=0 division or branch. The adapter has 34,252 scalar parameters; it does not change embedding directions or introduce a global affine reparameterization.

The proposed fresh panel is 256 Finance and 128 Pile unique natural source records, paired across `public_base` and `public_lora_2601`. That is 384 source records and 768 paired target observations, with 128 stored tokens including BOS and 127 scored post-BOS positions per record. Finance uses `[12000, 20000)` and Pile `[7000, 10000)`, preserving natural order and format. The proposed counts are within the prior capacity projection, but the current `source_inventory.json` is deliberately marked `IDENTITY_CAPACITY_PROJECTION_PENDING_FINAL_COUNT_ONLY_SCAN`: it subtracts the 1,024 Finance and 384 Pile TRR-0008 selected records from the prior eligible totals and therefore reports projected remaining capacity of 4,146 Finance and 364 Pile. It has not rescanned H128 duplicate effects under the latest exclusion union. A final count-only scan must apply the latest approved opaque exclusions before any selection.

The primary decision cell is Finance `public_base`, comparing adaptable readout with continued fixed readout. A useful pilot signal requires either an exact-record point difference of at least +2 percentage points with a conservative one-sided 97.5% CP lower bound above zero, or a token point difference of at least +0.25 percentage points with a one-sided 97.5% source-record bootstrap lower bound above zero. A lower bound above zero below the point floor is positive evidence only. Exact and token routes are the two predeclared primary routes; domains, target conditions, methods, and frequency bins are not pooled.

All four domain-target cells must clear exact harm of −5 percentage points and token harm of −1 percentage point using one-sided 95% bounds. The eight rare/absent gates are the two bins `0` and `1-4` in all four cells. Each requires at least 32 exposed source records and a one-sided 95% source-record bootstrap lower bound no worse than −1 token point. Natural token accuracy is computed as total correct positions divided by total exposed positions within each cell and bin, with numerator and denominator recomputed after source-record resampling; it is not an unweighted mean of per-record stratum accuracies when exposure counts differ. Insufficient support is `UNKNOWN`, never an automatic pass.

The improved-bank fitting frequency strata are fixed as published: 128,256 vocabulary IDs split into `0` (111,130 IDs, 0 occurrences), `1-4` (14,257 IDs, 22,783 occurrences), `5-9` (1,587 IDs, 10,165 occurrences), `10-49` (1,061 IDs, 19,463 occurrences), and `50+` (221 IDs, 71,960 occurrences), totaling 17,126 supported IDs and 124,371 post-BOS occurrences. These counts come from the published TRR-0007 `coverage_mix_v1` corpus plan and are not redefined for this task.

The cost gate is a warmed candidate/fixed runtime ratio of at most 1.25 in every cell under the same synchronization boundary. Adapter weights, optimizer state, preparation, training wall time, peak memory, and deployed footprint are reported separately. Both continued arms have the same fixed training schedule; a resource-guard failure stops the pilot and leaves the adaptation question unresolved. No automatic replacement, retraining, sample expansion, or new A2 run follows an outcome.

The exact CP sensitivity artifact is `power_analysis.json`. It uses the corrected CP upper tail (`beta.ppf(1-alpha_component, ...)`), a conditional discordance sensitivity `q=19/128`, and reports effects from 0.5 through 10 percentage points for Finance 128/256/384/512 and Pile 128/256. It is planning sensitivity only: candidate-versus-fixed discordance is unknown before truth, and token-route power is not claimed before its observed source-record variation is available.
