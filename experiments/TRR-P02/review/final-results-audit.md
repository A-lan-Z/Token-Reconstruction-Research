# TRR-P02 final results audit

## Scope and status

This is an independent review of the finalized public run at
`experiments/TRR-P02/runtime/cpu-public-geometry-20260905-run4/`. The run is
`COMPLETE_PUBLIC_DIAGNOSTIC` with `truth_opened=false` and
`source_truth_included=false`. It uses only public teacher prefixes, the
reused P01 prototype table, and the frozen historical lens. There was no new
fitting, decoder training, private target access, or full per-context vocabulary
table.

The panel is the predeclared compact design: T =
(13, 32, 198, 220, 2048, 4096, 16384, 29871), reference r=220, and seed
314159. C0 is `[BOS]` at endpoint position 1. C1--C4 are
`[BOS,13]`, `[BOS,198]`, `[BOS,1024]`, and `[BOS,29871]`, each with an
endpoint at position 2. C5 and C6 are repeated-13 controls with endpoints at
positions 3 and 4.

## Decision 1: wiring and cache execution

The reused P01 C0 rows were recomputed under the run's eight-thread CPU path.
After dtype matching, recomputation and the reused table are bitwise equal:
maximum and mean absolute difference are both 0.0.

The cache comparison has two distinct meanings. For every C0--C6 context, the
cached context block and the corresponding full-context output are exactly equal
in BF16 (maximum and mean absolute difference 0.0). Incremental cached endpoint
outputs are close but not bitwise equal: the largest maximum absolute
difference is 0.00390625 (C6), with a largest per-row mean absolute difference
of 0.00058006. The later endpoint probes used to test cache isolation have a
largest maximum absolute difference of 0.0078125 (C4) and a largest per-row mean
absolute difference of 0.00073707. Every persistent cache remained unchanged
during the probe.

Thus there is no gross cache-length, cache-isolation, or block-execution error
in this check, but the endpoint probes are not exact equality tests. The run
does not recompute rankings, RMS differences, or cosine differences from cached
versus full endpoint vectors. Cache is therefore unlikely to be the principal
explanation for the observed public-panel geometry, while a rank-level cache
equivalence claim would exceed the recorded evidence.

For reference wiring, the declared rule is
`q_minus = h - (z(C,r)-b_r)`, with r=220. Across C0--C4, the q-minus
self-consistency errors against recomputed `b_r` are 0.005859, 0.002441,
0.003906, 0.001953, and 0.002930 maximum absolute difference, respectively.
The opposite-sign q-plus control reaches 0.7393, 0.8418, 0.5977, and 1.0391
on C1--C4. This supports the subtraction sign. The reference probe itself is
not bitwise equal to the full endpoint (maximum 0.005859 across the primary
panel), so that result is a BF16 numerical check rather than an exact clone.

## Decision 2: wrong-prefix cascade versus public geometry

C1--C4 are teacher-prefix inputs with the endpoint at the same position and
the visible prefix supplied exactly. Their results cannot be generated by a
reconstructed-prefix cascade. They therefore provide a direct public
representation check separate from the causal failure cascade seen in P01.

The shared-offset definition is `delta(C,v)=z(C,v)-b_v`, where
`b_v=z(C0,v)`. For C1--C4, the residual-to-offset median ratios are 2.221,
2.889, 2.227, and 1.994; the aggregate mean over the 32 token/context
values is 2.315. This aggregate needs a sensitivity note: token 13 is a
large public-panel outlier, with offset norms 53.568, 54.146, 53.638, and
53.685 in C1--C4, while all other token offsets are between 1.778 and 4.070.
The non-outlier conclusion is still visible in pair deformation: excluding
token 13 leaves 84 C1--C4 pairs with relative deformation mean 0.881, median
0.873, and deformation-norm mean 3.909, median 3.906. Pairs involving token 13
have relative deformation near 1.0 and dominate absolute L2 summaries. The
{2048,4096} pair, which does not use that outlier, has relative deformations
0.737, 0.632, 0.727, and 0.903 across C1--C4.

This compact panel supports context-dependent token geometry and does not
support a single shared translation as a sufficient public explanation. It
does not establish the mechanism of every P01 error or generalize to the
vocabulary. The outlier and the small public panel make the conclusion a
bounded diagnostic decision.

## Decision 3: length and relative-position controls

C1--C4 are the equal-position comparison: one visible prefix token and endpoint
position 2. C0 is retained as the explicit position-1 baseline and is excluded
from equal-position aggregates. C5 and C6 reuse token 13 at endpoint positions
3 and 4 to expose context-length/relative-position changes. Their same-token to
different-token separation ratios are 0.480 in L2 and 0.314 in cosine distance,
but these rows change both visible content and endpoint position. They are
descriptive repeated-context controls, not pure absolute-position effects. No
uniform RoPE translation is treated as a context-length surrogate.

These controls follow the narrow rationale of the primary sources: RoFormer
shows that rotary q/k interactions depend on relative position while preserving
vector norms ([RoFormer, arXiv:2104.09864](https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.09864));
the Transformer paper defines causal attention in which each position combines
preceding-token content through q/k/v
([Attention Is All You Need, arXiv:1706.03762](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.03762)).
The citations justify matching endpoint position and retaining separate
teacher-prefix context controls; they do not supply a claim about this model's
reconstruction accuracy.

## Decision 4: rankings and frozen-lens geometry

All five targeted full-vocabulary variants use the same 12 predeclared rows and
aligned candidate IDs (C1--C4 crossed with endpoint IDs 220, 2048, and 4096).
Raw, subtraction, and oracle scores use the reused P01 prototype vectors; the
projected variant uses one frozen-lens transform of those vectors; A1 uses raw
public input embeddings. Ranks are strict cosine ranks,
`1 + count(score > true_score)`; margins below are median true-versus-best-other
margins in cosine-equivalent units.

| Variant | Full-V top-1 | Mean true rank | Median margin |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Raw boundary | 7/12 (58.3%) | 5.000 | 0.04194 |
| Reference subtraction | 8/12 (66.7%) | 1.833 | 0.03851 |
| Public mean-centered oracle | 0/12 (0.0%) | 1261.833 | -0.50581 |
| Projected frozen prototypes | 11/12 (91.7%) | 1.083 | 0.15206 |
| Historical frozen A1 | 9/12 (75.0%) | 1.417 | 0.12226 |

The reference result should not be described as a general correction gain.
Four of its rows are self-reference rows with token 220 and margins near 0.509;
among the eight non-reference rows, raw gets 5/8 top-1 and subtraction gets 4/8.
The reference subtraction has a smaller worst rank (7) than the raw
variant's worst rank (28), but the aggregate is still driven by the trivial
self-reference rows and does not rescue the non-reference cases.

The restricted N=9 dictionary is explicitly a control, not a full-vocabulary
score: raw 35/40 top-1, reference subtraction 36/40, public mean-centered
oracle 40/40, and opposite sign 30/40. The oracle's 40/40 restricted result
alongside 0/12 full-vocabulary top-1 is direct evidence that restricted
rankings cannot establish a full-vocabulary method claim.

The frozen-lens comparison is also a geometry diagnostic. On the equal-position
C1--C4 rows, raw same-token/different-token L2 means are 3.3608/4.1702
(ratio 0.8059); projected means are 4.3330/5.9581 (ratio 0.7272). Raw
same-token/different-token cosine-distance means are 0.4820/0.7070 (ratio
0.6818); projected means are 0.4653/0.8558 (ratio 0.5437). The projection
raises both absolute L2 scales while lowering the same/different ratio. The
evidence supports a modest relative-separation improvement on this public
panel, not absolute L2 invariance.

The projection uses one streamed frozen-lens transform of the reused P01
prototypes. A1 uses raw public input embeddings through the original frozen
`FrozenAffineLens.forward`. A1 native scores are cosine times
`exp(s)=35.0647507`; the reported A1 ranks and margins divide by this positive
scale to make the scores and margins cosine-equivalent; ranks are unchanged by
this positive scaling, while native values remain in the JSON. For example, the
A1 median true-other margin is 0.12226 cosine-equivalent
(4.28687 in native logit units; mean native margin 3.56329).
Both are labelled frozen fitted-lens diagnostics, not fitting-free methods.
Their improved ranks do not show that external context or position features
are required, and do not establish a universal learned-decoder requirement.

## Mechanism disposition and stopping condition

The static, no-fit shared-offset explanation should be stopped for this
bounded question. The residuals and non-outlier pair deformation show that
context changes token relations, and the full-vocabulary reference result does
not improve the non-reference rows. The public mean-centered oracle is not a
deployable method and is decisively harmful on the same full-vocabulary rows.

The projected-prototype and A1 results are useful clues about a learned
geometry, but they are fitted comparator evidence only. A concrete next
mechanism for a separately registered future public check is one-pass low-rank
compression of the frozen affine-lens/projected-prototype scoring geometry,
using a predeclared compact rank and score budget. This tests whether the
observed projected ranking gain can be retained cheaply; it does not assume
that low-rank structure exists. P02 performs no such compression, fitting, or
model run. The check must preserve the full-vocabulary ranking rule and retain the
fitted-origin label. Stop this compression line if the bounded check loses the
projected top-1 or margin gain relative to raw under that predeclared budget;
do not start a duplicate decoder-training campaign. Any broader decoder
mechanism claim still needs a separately registered public evaluation with
held-out contexts and a declared scoring scope; no such fitting or rescue
experiment is part of P02.

## Limits and presentation

The evidence is a small public panel: eight token IDs, four equal-position
contexts, two repeated-13 controls, one 12-row full-vocabulary check, and N=9
restricted controls. It contains no private truth, benchmark score, full
per-context vocabulary table, or cached rank comparison. C5/C6 confound added
visible content with relative position. The token-13 outlier dominates absolute
L2 means and needs to remain visible in interpretation.

The runtime `lens_geometry.png` stacks unrelated same-token cross-context and
different-token within-context L2 quantities on one absolute scale. That
presentation is misleading for the comparison above. A compact grouped ratio
and ranking-summary derivative from `diagnostics.json` is preferable. The
posthoc derivative is `experiments/TRR-P02/review/summary_geometry.png`, made
by `derive_summary_figure.py` from the finalized JSON only; the runtime figure
is retained as provenance and should not be silently rewritten.
