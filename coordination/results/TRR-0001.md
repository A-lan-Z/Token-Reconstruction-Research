## Preregistered design

The committed plan used
`meta-llama/Llama-3.2-1B-Instruct@9213176726f574b556790deb65791e0c5aa438b6`
as both the pinned public base and the separately obtained public surrogate.
The evaluator trained a rank-4, alpha-8 LoRA on public, non-blind records,
affecting q/v projections in prefix layers 0–3 for 40 fixed steps. The target
LoRA and callable target prefix remained evaluator-only.

The pinned text source was
`NeelNanda/pile-10k@127bfedcd5047750df5ccf3a12979a47bfa0bafa`.
Deterministic disjoint splits contained 64 target-update, 128 inverse-training,
32 development, and 64 blind records. Each blind record contributed 39
non-BOS, non-padding tokens, for 2,496 tokens per condition/cut arm.

The baseline families were:

1. A positionwise direct inverse: identity at cut 0 and residual affine
   2048-to-2048 public-data inverses at cuts 4 and 8, followed by full-vocabulary
   cosine scoring.
2. A causal public-surrogate search: the direct inverse's frozen top-16
   proposals were simulated with the public prefix using BOS and only already
   committed reconstructed tokens; the lowest activation-distance candidate was
   committed.

Both methods used the same blind records and candidate budget, reconstructed
all positions, never abstained, and performed no cross-record adaptation.
Cut 4 was primary, cut 0 was an embedding-boundary sanity check, and cut 8 was
the preregistered depth stress test. Statistical intervals used 10,000 paired
record bootstrap draws with seed 1732.

The historical strict-BOS comparator was audited as
`RUNNABLE_BUT_INCOMPATIBLE_WITH_DECLARED_CONDITION`. Its exact 128×128
geometry, three-episode prior-trace adaptation contract, and historical target
traces do not match this 64×40 Pile/LoRA condition; changing those contracts
would not run the frozen historical comparator.

## Measured results

All 64 records and 2,496 tokens per arm were scored. There were no failed,
excluded, or abstained records in the final comparison.

|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| matched public | 0 | 100.00% | 100.00% | 0.00 | [0.00, 0.00] | 100.00% | 100.00% |
| matched public | 4 | 64.14% | 83.69% | +19.55 | [+17.43, +21.88] | 85.02% | 98.44% |
| matched public | 8 | 49.20% | 68.87% | +19.67 | [+17.39, +22.00] | 74.20% | 92.82% |
| unavailable target | 0 | 100.00% | 100.00% | 0.00 | [0.00, 0.00] | 100.00% | 100.00% |
| unavailable target | 4 | 64.94% | 85.82% | +20.87 | [+18.83, +23.08] | 86.46% | 99.26% |
| unavailable target | 8 | 49.20% | 68.31% | +19.11 | [+16.95, +21.27] | 73.60% | 92.81% |

At the primary unavailable-target cut, causal token accuracy was 85.82% versus
64.94% direct. The +20.87 percentage-point paired gain has a 95% bootstrap
interval that excludes zero, so the preregistered rule supports a quality
improvement at the shared candidate budget. The analogous non-embedding gains
are supported in all four target/control cut-4/cut-8 comparisons.

Exact sequence match was 100% at cut 0. At the primary cut it was 0/64 for the
direct inverse and 2/64 (3.125%) for causal search. It was 0/64 for both methods
in the other non-embedding arms. These low sequence rates show that the
token-level gain does not establish reliable full-sequence recovery.

H1 is supported within this scope: both positionwise and causal accuracy
decreased from cut 4 to cut 8. H2 is supported within this scope: conditional
causal ranking substantially exceeded direct top-1 selection, and the paired
overall gain was supported. H3 is not supported as a general degradation:
target-minus-matched causal accuracy at cut 4 was unexpectedly +2.12 points
(95% CI +1.08 to +3.17), while the direct difference and both cut-8 differences
were inconclusive. H4 is not the dominant explanation here. H5 separated the
dominant proposal failure from the smaller ranking and propagation effects.

## Post-truth diagnostics

At the primary unavailable-target cut, frozen top-16 recall was 86.46% while
conditional causal selection accuracy was 99.26%. The teacher-prefix
counterfactual reached 86.46%, only 0.64 percentage points above the frozen
reconstructed-prefix result of 85.82%. This identifies candidate proposal as
the dominant measured bottleneck, with little residual ranking loss and only a
small effect from replacing reconstructed with true prefixes.

At cut 8, top-16 recall was 73.60%, conditional causal selection was 92.81%,
and the teacher-prefix counterfactual was 73.08% versus 68.31% under frozen
reconstructed prefixes. Proposal remains the larger ceiling, although prefix
propagation becomes more material at the deeper cut.

The primary causal accuracy strictly after each record's first error was
85.65%, close to its 85.82% aggregate accuracy. Errors therefore did not trigger
a severe collapse at cut 4. Position, public-frequency, token-group, exact-rank,
and correct/incorrect activation-score diagnostics are committed in
`experiments/TRR-0001/diagnostics.json` and
`experiments/TRR-0001/per_record_metrics.jsonl`.

## Failed attempts and deviations

1. The first evaluator attempt emitted a Flash Attention nondeterminism warning.
   That entire pre-truth run was excluded and moved to
   `outputs/TRR-0001-excluded-flash-warning`; committed logs preserve the
   warning. Deterministic SDPA execution was then enforced before the valid run.
2. A determinism check compared private truth-sidecar bytes before freeze.
   It revealed that all tensors and semantic metadata matched while
   safetensors header map ordering made raw bytes differ. Because even a
   byte-only comparison was a procedural truth read, both affected production
   and reproduction attempts were excluded in full and retained under
   `outputs/TRR-0001-excluded-pre-gate-read` and
   `outputs/TRR-0001-repro-excluded-pre-gate-read`. The final run restarted
   from fixed code and made no pre-freeze truth access.
3. The first reconstruction attempt exhausted GPU memory when forking caches
   for 64 records at once. It was pre-truth, produced no completed blind
   comparison, and is retained as `frozen_bundle-excluded-oom` within the
   excluded root. Cache batching was bounded at 16 records without changing the
   candidate budget or causal semantics, and the complete blind run was
   restarted.
4. The first post-truth scorer correctly preserved all frozen predictions and
   quality metrics but reported causal selection time without adding direct
   proposal time. Its outputs are committed under
   `experiments/TRR-0001/excluded-score-attempt1`. The scoring code was
   corrected after truth opening only for additive runtime accounting; no
   prediction, candidate, route, quality value, or blind decision changed.
5. The exact historical comparator was not run for the preregistered condition
   because its fixed assets are runnable but its geometry and adaptation
   contract are incompatible. Its successful public-teacher identity check,
   hashes, and synthetic preflight remain in the resource audit.
6. The causal cache implementation processes 16 records per chunk rather than
   the initially attempted 64 to fit the available 16,303 MiB GPU. Candidate
   count, order, public-prefix semantics, comparison arms, and claim scope are
   unchanged.
7. The Windows workspace patch helper failed setup refresh, so repository-local
   `git apply` was used for handoff edits after the required helper attempt.
   This editing-path deviation has no scientific effect.

No blind answer was used to select, tune, reroute, stop, or revise an evaluated
output.

## Claims supported

- Under the exact evaluated scope and shared 16-candidate budget, causal
  public-surrogate selection improves token accuracy over the direct inverse at
  cuts 4 and 8.
- Recoverability is lower at cut 8 than cut 4 for both baseline families.
- Candidate proposal is the highest-value measured bottleneck at the primary
  cut; causal ranking is nearly perfect conditional on inclusion.
- The embedding-boundary sanity condition reconstructs exactly.
- The evaluator/reconstructor separation and immutable freeze gate operated
  successfully in the final valid run.

## Claims not supported

- Reliable exact full-sequence reconstruction at non-embedding cuts.
- A general claim that unavailable-prefix mismatch reduces recovery; this
  particular LoRA condition did not show that pattern consistently.
- Generalization to other models, datasets, target updates, cuts, sequence
  lengths, candidate budgets, or inversion families.
- A Pareto improvement: the causal baseline improved quality but cost more.
- A conclusion that prefix propagation is irrelevant at deeper cuts.
- Compatibility or superiority relative to the historical strict-BOS run.

## Limitations

This is one 1B-parameter decoder model, one evaluator-only rank-4 target update,
one public dataset, 64 blind records, fixed 39-token sequences, three cuts, one
inverse architecture, and one candidate budget. The same public base underlies
the surrogate and target update, so stronger target mismatch may behave
differently. Bootstrap uncertainty reflects records from this fixed selected
sample, not model- or update-level variation. Large frozen activations, model
weights, and private truth are retained locally rather than committed; their
paths, hashes, byte counts, immutable source IDs, and regeneration commands are
recorded in the manifest.

## Recommended next research action

Propose—but do not start—a new preregistered task that improves public-only
candidate proposal recall at cuts 4 and 8 while holding the validated causal
selector fixed. It should evaluate multiple candidate budgets and report the
quality-cost frontier on a fresh blind split, preferably across multiple
evaluator-only target updates. This directly attacks the 13.54-point primary
top-16 proposal miss rate and tests whether added proposal cost converts into
end-to-end accuracy before investing in more elaborate ranking or adaptation.
