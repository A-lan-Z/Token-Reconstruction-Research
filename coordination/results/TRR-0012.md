# TRR-0012 — New-weight fixed-readout replication

## Current result

The main finding is that the expanded public support improves B1 over B0 in all four released cells: token gains are +0.5813 to +3.9801 percentage points, with no exact-record losses in the paired inventory. The remaining limitation is comparative: B1 is still below the frozen A1 first-128 subset, especially on Pile exact records. This reproduces an expanded-support gain for one new-weight pair without establishing seed reliability or canonical promotion.

The native TRR-P09 fixed-control runner completed the one authorized new-weight B0/B1 pair from the frozen common contract. B0 selected step 8,000 and B1 selected step 13,000 under the earliest strict maximum rule on the seven-point public-validation grid. Both selected states were packaged, copied to Windows, and independently restored; selected-state, readout, and four-record smoke identities matched. The retained package is frozen and restorable. The released unused-source confirmation covers four cells, 256 records per cell, 32,512 scored post-BOS tokens per cell, 512 unique sources, and the first-128-per-domain A1 comparator. It was scored only after the complete prediction freeze. The separate expanded-B1 transfer diagnostic is also scored and frozen across 14 cells. This completes the bounded research handoff; PR23 remains draft and unmerged. The confirmation cost and frozen proposal-rank receipts are bound below. A1 proposal ranks are available within 512 candidates; the true-token rank under A2 simulated-prefix cosine scores is unavailable.

The historical TRR-0011 exact confirmation remains incomplete because its selected artifacts were lost; this run does not rescue it or establish seed reliability, canonical promotion, or benchmark-complete status.

## Geometry and interpretation

The four prefix variants measurably displaced internal features while changing zero prediction rows. Their mean relative raw-activation L2 ranges from about 0.0030 to 0.0106, with maxima about 0.0063 to 0.0268. Their margin-normalized feature-shift means range from about 0.0138 to 0.0791, with maxima from about 0.871 to 10.15. The null is exactly zero on the corresponding displacement fields.

A feature displacement divided by the clean top-versus-runner boundary distance can exceed one without crossing that boundary. It is not a signed crossing test or a nearest-class certificate. The zero flips in artificial cells with maxima up to 10.15 illustrate this distinction. The near-one historical AUC is exploratory ranking evidence from one historical condition and five broken token events; it does not establish independent-target generalization, calibration, or global transfer robustness.

## Deviations and overhead

The restored runtime adds a disclosed one-line missing-constant alias; all four already-opened fixture outputs matched the original source with the same alias. Two initial separate-diagnostic launches failed before model loading: one rejected external sanitized observation metadata, and one lacked the frozen runtime source modules under `PYTHONPATH=.`. Both outputs are preserved and excluded. After the public prediction freeze, the evaluator callback encountered a technical post-truth storage-shape mismatch. The deterministic repair flattened `[32,127,2,2048]` to `[4064,2,2048]`; API, truth order, metric definitions, and fixed thresholds were unchanged, with no new inference, selection, or fitting.

The first private rank-diagnostic byte stream was observed as 14,044 bytes with hash `0671acf3…` but was overwritten during helper regeneration and was not recovered. The released provenance receipt records this exception; the regenerated 11,060-byte artifact was rebuilt from unchanged frozen traces, predictions, freeze metadata, and post-freeze truth inputs, with known per-cell metrics preserved and no inference rerun. The original intermediate must not be described as retained.

Operationally, qualify the cold entrypoint and scorer together on a synthetic shape fixture, assign one owner and artifact per phase, and release the GPU during deterministic repairs. Preserve create-only outputs, hashes, and truth gates; reuse evidence unless code/inputs change, delegate by phase, and reserve compute for heavy or comparative work.

## Next experiment and change-of-direction criterion

The next bounded hypothesis is public hard-example/support sampling against the existing sampler on the same expanded bank, with the same update budget with the readout frozen, evaluated on a newly reserved unopened evaluation panel to test whether it reduces the Pile exact-record gap. The current confirmation panel is opened and retained only as historical evidence. The released Pile B0-unseen strata motivate this test, but do not prove a frequency mechanism or make target mismatch the dominant limitation. Before any new selection, freeze the sampling rule, analysis bins, and useful-gain criterion. If improved fitting occurs but an adequately precise new-source comparison excludes the preregistered useful gain, move toward a capacity/readout/optimization diagnosis rather than further same-sampling expansion; if the comparison is underpowered or inconclusive, report that explicitly.
