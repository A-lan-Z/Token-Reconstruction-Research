# TRR-0004 — Public coverage, bias, and contextual decoder pilot

**Status:** public development and the fresh five-method × four-cell matrix are complete. The canonical dual benchmark remains **NOT RUN**, and these results make no replacement or independent broad target-transfer claim.

## What we learned

A larger, controlled public fit closed most of the earlier standalone-decoder gap. With the same 3,000 optimizer updates, the no-bias affine fit rose from 74.3377% aggregate public-validation accuracy on 5,000 nested post-BOS fitting positions to 94.3505% on 124,371 positions. Style-balanced accuracy rose from 68.3650% to 91.7100%, with fit times of 93.0 s and 94.4 s. The larger result closely matches retained A1's 94.3185% on this public validation panel.

The small fits reached 100% accuracy on their stored fitting rows, while the large fits reached 99.9992%. The small models selected step 150 on validation and deteriorated with longer training; the large models selected step 1900. Under this recipe, these observations point to public-data amount/diversity/coverage and held-out generalization as the main remaining fitting limitation. They do not isolate token coverage from corpus diversity or explain every difference from the old retained asset. The controlled vocabulary-bias effect was much smaller than the small-to-large data effect.

The controlled fit records were all run for 3,000 updates with the same declared fitting procedure:

The retained historical A1 state is 16,787,653 bytes, but its exact original fitting records and elapsed fitting cost are unavailable; the documented recipe is available. Reusing that state therefore must not be reported as zero preparation cost; the current fit times above apply only to the new controlled fits.

The large-fit coverage diagnostic shows a persistent seen/unseen difference: frequent labels (fit frequency at least 65) scored 1772/1780 (99.5506%), while fit-absent labels scored 262/382 (68.5864%). This is a public matched-data diagnostic; target-weight transfer remains a separate question.

The fresh public confirmation is now scored after a complete prediction freeze. On 5,312 post-BOS tokens and 64 record-conditions (32 unique source records paired across two target conditions), the retained A1 direct decoder scored 4948/5312 (93.1476%) with 0 exact records, the no-bias affine decoder scored 4910/5312 (92.4322%) with 0 exact records, and the causal attention extension scored 4943/5312 (93.0535%) with 2 exact records. Attention improved over the fresh affine baseline by 33 tokens across the four cells; the matched MLP improved by 7 tokens and had 0 exact records. These gains are small and panel-specific.

A2 remained the quality comparator: it scored 5308/5312 (99.9247%) and 60/64 exact records. It is a CPU-normalized numerical port for this run; native historical GPU normalization equivalence was not established. Its quality comes with a large inference cost shown below.

## Data, access, and method

The public fit used 1,200 Alpaca records and 124,371 post-BOS positions; the stored bundle contains 125,571 rows including one BOS row per record. The nested control used 5,000 post-BOS positions (5,043 stored rows including BOS). Validation used 24 Alpaca and 24 Pile records with 3,133 post-BOS positions, disjoint from fitting. Affine selection used the unweighted mean of the two public style accuracies. Contextual extensions froze the selected competent affine base, used the same fixed normalization and position sampler, and added about 1.05 million parameters each.

Public activations use the qualified batch-8 × 192 cut-4 execution, cropped to each evaluation length. Active outputs were unchanged by future padding; the non-equivalent unpadded batch-1 diagnostic was excluded.

The fresh quality panel has 16 Pile and 16 Finance source records. Each is observed under public_base and public_lora_2601, so the 64 record-conditions are 32 distinct source records paired across the two conditions. public_lora_2601 is one synthetic LoRA target-shift diagnostic, not full target-shift coverage.

The standalone decoders consume the captured activation, the normalized public embedding table, and their selected state. They use zero public-prefix calls, zero candidate simulations, and no A2 fallback. The producer separately read selected public texts plus public base/LoRA resources to create the frozen panel and public truth sidecar. The reconstructor did not read source text, target weights, or truth. The scorer opened the truth sidecar only after the complete 20-artifact public gate and used it only for declared metrics. Full hashes, exact commands, and access scopes are in experiments/TRR-0004/manifest.json.

## Coverage and position diagnostics

The score JSON's marginal frequency bins (0, 1–4, 5–19, 20+) show stronger results for frequent labels and weakest results for frequency-zero labels across the methods. The position bins (1–15, 16–39, 40–79, 80+) show that the small method differences are position-dependent. These are diagnostics from the declared public coverage reference and do not by themselves establish the cause of those errors.

On Finance public_base, A1 has a 16-token advantage over affine. The separate score marginals place this edge in the frequency-zero (“unseen”) and position 16–39 bins; they do not provide a joint cross-tab, so “unseen mid-position” is a diagnostic interpretation rather than a record-level causal attribution. On Pile, attention's +1.6026 and +1.7628 percentage-point gains over affine in the two target conditions are concentrated in the position 16–39 marginal (9 of 10 and 7 of 11 tokens respectively). The exact Pile record named above is the one exact-record gain in both conditions. These findings support testing whether the gain from the contextual extension persists after improving public coverage; the MLP comparison alone does not isolate access to earlier activations from every architectural difference.

## Public fitting and bias controls

Vocabulary bias has a smaller, partial post-hoc effect. Zeroing an already fitted bias changed the tied affine result from 587/936 (62.7137%) to 602/936 (64.3162%), and the residual MLP from 506/936 (54.0598%) to 540/936 (57.6923%). Bias deletion after fitting is not evidence for a no-bias fitting procedure. Scale and normalization controls were inference-only: changing fitted scale from 16 to 1 reduced tied and MLP results to 429/936 and 416/936, with zero unseen hits. The bridge control was bit-exact for projection/logits and top-1/top-512 ranks on 960 public rows with zero mismatches. These are implementation and mechanism diagnostics.

## Contextual extensions and costs

Causal attention selected public checkpoint step 25 and gained 12 raw public-validation tokens (+0.383 percentage points aggregate and +0.518 style-balanced); the positionwise MLP selected step 1200 and gained one token. Both selected models were trained for 3,000 updates with the same public sampler and selected on public validation. The final full-fit evaluations are selection-independent capacity diagnostics. The tiny eight-record subset was already at 100.0000% and 8/8 at step 0, so it is not evidence of new capacity or optimization.

Public activation preparation v2 took 9.764 s. The selected large affine fit took 94.4 s (3,000 updates); the attention phase took an additional 89.080 s including validation and its 3.388 s final evaluation. The MLP phase took 85.565 s including its 3.282 s final evaluation. Selected contextual states are approximately 20.99 MB each (20,990,628 and 20,989,748 bytes); the normalized public embedding table is 1.05 GB (1,050,673,488 bytes) and dominates the standalone retained runtime footprint. These are preparation and fitting costs; the fresh matrix's full-run and steady inference costs are separate above. The retained historical A1 fitting cost remains unknown. The eight-record attention/MLP checks added 16.483/16.158 s; qualifier and failed-attempt costs are preserved separately. The embedding table is a reused public artifact, and its original table-only preparation time is not separately established.

## Exclusions and limits

Preserved exclusions include public activation preparation v1, affine qualification v1, contextual qualification wrappers v1/v2, superseded contextual qualifier v4 (not run; it contains malformed duplicated validation-artifact digest metadata), the first coverage attempt, the A2 v1 CPU/GPU normalized-embedding mismatch, the affine wrong-selection-path launch, and the incomplete A1/affine v1 four-cell runs. The accepted v2 preparation, v2 prediction matrix, and truth-gated score are separate artifacts. All failed and excluded attempts have been preserved in the manifest/evidence records. An independent P03 setup message was accidentally routed to this task; no P03 files, results, predictions, or labels were received, read, or used.

This round does not establish canonical dual-benchmark comparability, broad target transfer, or a replacement method. The panel has 16 Pile and 16 Finance records, paired across public-base and public-LoRA conditions. The target shift is one synthetic diagnostic, and public fit coverage remains confounded with data diversity.

## Decision

The next experiment should be one bounded 2×2 comparison: lower versus higher public token-ID coverage at a fixed fitting-position budget, crossed with affine versus the same causal attention extension. Match corpus style and length distributions, keep rendering, affine architecture, optimizer recipe, and update budgets fixed, and choose the public fitting corpora without using this confirmation panel's labels. Refit each affine base, then apply the same zero-initialized attention extension and evaluate a new independent panel. This tests whether improving public coverage makes the contextual gain redundant or whether both improvements remain useful; any residual corpus-diversity confounding must be reported.

Prioritize this search-free path because the controlled affine fit is now competent and the contextual extension gave a small gain in every fresh cell. Change direction toward coverage alone if the better-covered affine base improves while the attention gain disappears. Continue the contextual extension only if its incremental token and exact-record gains replicate with useful paired uncertainty bounds and acceptable fitting/state cost. Canonical dual-benchmark confirmation remains necessary before a replacement claim.
