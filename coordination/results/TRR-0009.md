# TRR-0009: final bounded readout-adaptation result

TRR-0009 produces **no useful pilot signal under the preregistered decision rule**. The primary Finance `public_base` adaptable-versus-fixed contrast is +0.7813 percentage points exact (3 gains, 1 loss; 79/256 versus 77/256) and +0.024606 percentage points in token accuracy (8 additional correct positions; 31,957/32,512 versus 31,949/32,512). Both point estimates miss the useful floors of +2 exact points and +0.25 token points. The 97.5% lower bounds are −2.2808 exact points and −0.003076 token points. The token upper bound is +0.055364 points, below the +0.25 token floor, so the registered token-sized benefit is excluded. The exact upper bound remains +3.7497 points, so a +2-point exact benefit is unresolved rather than excluded. The cost, exact-harm, token-harm, and all eight rare/absent safeguards pass. The frozen action is to retain the reference; this does not establish that adaptation is harmful.

Truth was opened only after the public freeze and the explicitly authorized narrow metadata repair. The completed score is [`score_v2.json`](../../experiments/TRR-0009/evaluation/score_v2.json), and the deterministic outcome is [`aggregate_decision_v2.json`](../../experiments/TRR-0009/evaluation/aggregate_decision_v2.json). Full commands, hashes, failed attempts, and execution receipts remain in the [reproducibility appendix](TRR-0009_reproducibility_appendix.md); the structured task manifest is [`experiments/TRR-0009/manifest.json`](../../experiments/TRR-0009/manifest.json).

## Frozen question and panel

The primary question was whether a supported-token readout adaptation improves reconstruction over an equally continued fixed-readout control. The adaptable arm applies `logit_v = base_logit_v * gain_v + bias_v` to the 17,126 supported token IDs; absent IDs remain fixed. The primary cell is Finance `public_base`, with Pile and `public_lora_2601` as secondary descriptive routes. The retained published state is a historical comparison, not a refit.

The corrected panel contains 256 Finance and 128 Pile natural source records, paired across two target conditions: 384 unique records and 768 target observations, with 127 post-BOS positions per record. Finance uses `[12000,20000)` and Pile `[7000,10000)`. The repaired identity-only selection retained the frozen seed, ranges, natural ordering, and counts after the first panel was excluded for one P08 H128 overlap. No source text or labels are present in selection artifacts. Scores use source-record pairing, 10,000 bootstrap draws, seed 9009, and no pooled domains or target conditions.

A useful primary route required an exact point improvement of at least 2 points or a token point improvement of at least 0.25 points, together with its declared 97.5% lower bound above zero. Every cell also had to pass exact harm ≥−5 points, token harm ≥−1 point, and adaptable/fixed cost ≤1.25. Rare/absent bins required at least 32 exposed source records and a 95% lower bound ≥−1 token point.

## Gate repair, truth boundary, and decision

The initial public gate failed before truth because the continued-fixed state omitted two redundant header booleans even though its exact state hash, schema, geometry, inference contract, and loader flags were bound. The authorized exact-checkpoint maintenance adapter accepted only that state and preserved the fail-closed downstream checks. A first truth-preparation attempt materialized selected labels in RAM and then failed while serializing the frozen tokenizer directory; it wrote no sidecar, binding, or score. That excluded failure is recorded separately. The repaired source adapter then prepared the private sidecar and the score completed with `truth_opened=true`; raw truth remains outside committed artifacts. The score execution used full code commit `16e86a5` and 10,000 source-record bootstrap draws.

The aggregate status is `FAIL` only because the primary useful-pilot gate fails its practical point-floor/interval rule; exact harm, token harm, rare/absent support, cost, and alias checks all pass. Numeric review v2 is authoritative over its superseded v1 and records the same correction: pooled arithmetic is descriptive, token useful benefit is excluded by its per-cell upper bounds, and exact practical benefit remains unresolved. If a follow-up is justified, first freeze the method, support set, code, inputs, thresholds, and an independent fresh panel before opening its truth. Then run one regularized token-specific directional correction at the same public bank and schedule with a matched fixed control and the same four-cell safeguards. This separates restricted-direction capacity from scalar calibration. The learning curve reached a maximum raw gain of 0.009505 and raw bias of 0.000841; these are tanh-parameter values rather than direct effective-bound values, and provide no evidence that the current ±0.25 effective gain/bias bounds constrained learning, so they should not be widened. The reversal criterion is unchanged: only a future preregistered useful primary signal with all safeguards passing could change the retained-reference decision. Do not promote, retrain, or expand the sample automatically, and do not treat this result as support for a global linear reparameterization that the decoder could absorb.
