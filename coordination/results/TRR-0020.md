## Completed development7–9 and direct-score follow-up
All three matrices froze completely before current retrospective scoring and are hash-verified/archived. Dev7 (64cells) tested Gini penalties and straight-through hardening. Mild penalties accelerate some matched cases; best half-hard whole-objective output gives matched254/254 natural,78/78identifiers and shifted252/254natural,78/78identifiers. Full hardening can diverge severely and is rejected. No speed/accuracy replacement established.

Dev8 (104cells) tested categorical-gradient preconditioning against an independent dense Fisher identity; no adequate improvement. Dev9 (56cells) tested parallel conditional soft tokens against current hard reconstructed contexts. An independent tiny-model test verified forward values and gradients against five separate native references, including zero cross-position gradients. The method worsened prose accuracy and is rejected.

Dev10 now tests a different calculation: one hard-sequence prefix evaluation and each position's own gradient produce a local quadratic score for every vocabulary token. A full-vocabulary argmax updates the sequence directly; no candidate shortlist is forwarded for verification. The extra128256-dimensional soft optimizer is removed. This approximation is unproven and remains an exploratory test. Original no-shortlist objective remains active; no canonical replacement claim.
