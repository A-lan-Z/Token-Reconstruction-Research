# TRR-0003 — Initial probes for simpler token reconstruction

**Status:** exploratory development panel scored after the prediction freeze; canonical dual benchmark **not run**. The 52-cell receipt is complete, but this pilot does not promote a replacement, accept TRR-0002, merge PR #3, or change the active method registry. The last accepted task remains TRR-0001 and PR #3 remains unmerged.

## What we learned

The two probes gave different kinds of negative evidence.

Track A tested a genuine checkpoint-only structural inverse: a damped reverse fixed-point map over the public cut-4 pre-norm residual blocks, followed by exact public embedding projection. It stayed finite, but the matched public-base control did not converge as a composed inverse. Its continuous cycle residual was non-monotonic and remained 1.406–1.777 at iteration 32 across the four panel cells. The same pattern appeared under the shifted target. This is a demonstrated failure of this numerical solver on its matched control; it is not evidence that every checkpoint-only inverse is impossible. The panel token result also collapses with iteration count, reaching 0–3.526% at iteration 16 and 0–2.244% at iteration 32 depending on style and condition.

Track B tested standalone direct token prediction with no A2 fallback. The selected angular, tied-affine, and residual-MLP decoders reached 65.223–71.154%, 63.911–68.269%, and 53.150–57.692% on the four development cells, with no completely reconstructed records. The historical public-Alpaca A1 comparator reached 91.987–95.932% without A2 on the same panel, so this first standalone fit does not close the gap to the strongest existing search-free comparator. The tiny public subset fit reached 100% for all three arms, which separates basic optimization and wiring from held-out transfer failure. Broader public coverage and prespecified stopping remain open variables.

The strongest comparator remained frozen A1+A2 at 99.679–100.000%, with 7/8 exact records on Pile/public-base and 8/8 on the other three cells. It is retained as a reference for the accuracy gap and still uses candidate-by-candidate public-prefix simulation. The new methods therefore leave two bounded next questions: whether a better-conditioned structural solver can pass its matched control, and whether broader public supervision lifts a search-free decoder beyond the current transfer ceiling.

## Registered methods and fixed rules

Track A registered checkpoint_reverse_fixed_point_euclidean_k16. It initializes each estimate at the observed boundary activation, reverses layers 3 through 0 in MLP-then-attention order, uses damping 0.5, and performs two branch evaluations per fixed-point step. The projection is exact squared-Euclidean nearest neighbor over the public embedding table with top-K 16 diagnostics, vocabulary chunks of 8,192, and deterministic token-ID tie breaking. Its predeclared ladder is iterations 0, 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, and 32. Iteration 0 is the explicit boundary-to-embedding identity baseline. Records run one at a time after stripping only right padding and require position IDs equal to arange(valid_length). It makes zero candidate-prefix simulations.

Track B compared the existing angular inverse control with a tied affine decoder and a 256-wide residual MLP decoder. The CE arms use full-vocabulary CE, normalized tied public embeddings, logit scale 16, AdamW, learning rate 0.001, batch size 512, gradient clipping 1.0, and the predeclared seed. The extension to 1,800 steps was made before panel prediction; selected checkpoints were then replayed using the public validation curve and the earliest-best rule. Runtime emits direct argmax tokens at every active position, with no candidate list, public-prefix call, target weights, or A2 fallback.

## Forward-path and BOS control

The initial public identity control used batch-8 execution and matched the cached cut-4 activation on 24 disjoint public validation records with max_abs 0 and relative L2 0. The follow-up v2 control used two of those records with the same batch-one forward_full path used to form the fresh matched target. On both records, token forward and embedding-space forward remained bit-exact with each other (max_abs 0). Both differed from cached H4 by 1.276% and 1.557% post-BOS relative L2. This is evidence of a batch and activation-generation numerical difference under the two paths; it means the earlier identity result does not justify excluding numerical input differences.

The v2 inverse control ran the same i32/damping-0.5 solver against the cached H4 target and against a target generated by the same batch-one forward_full call:

| target | record 0 post-BOS residual before → after BOS clamp | record 1 post-BOS residual before → after BOS clamp | BOS embedding error before clamp |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| cached H4 | 0.120633 → 0.273051 | 0.498779 → 0.679374 | 892.378 relative L2 |
| same batch-one public forward | 0.117105 → 0.271971 | 0.582166 → 0.676518 | 904.332 relative L2 |

The fresh matched target therefore still has a substantial residual before and after clamping. This separates two observations: numerical/cache or batching differences are material and must be retained as a limitation, while the fixed-point inverse also fails on a target generated by its own public forward path. The BOS embedding error itself is removed by the clamp, but its causal effect worsens post-BOS residual in these records. For the cached target the changes are +0.152419 and +0.180595; for the fresh matched target they are +0.154866 and +0.094351.

The v2 run made 1,024 inverse branch calls per record across the two targets, six complete prefix forwards, and 24 full-prefix layer evaluations; branch calls and complete-prefix evaluations are reported separately. It made zero candidate simulations and opened no panel truth. Its receipt is outputs/TRR-0003/track_a_bos_forward_diagnostic/public_bos_forward_v2.json (SHA-256 72ffa41c3d6b78f4240f57c4b40cb9def8193bacb69762c365049c85e30f952b).

## Limitations, failed attempts, and provenance

The primary Track A limitation is solver optimization and conditioning, demonstrated by non-convergent composed residuals on matched public controls. The fresh same-path control supports that interpretation but does not remove a real batch/cache numerical discrepancy. Target-weight mismatch is not established as the cause because the matched inverse already fails; the shifted cells are a paired diagnostic, not a proof about unknown targets. Projection quality is also poor, with discrete residuals near one. Runtime grows with the ladder, making a costly online solver unattractive unless convergence improves.

Track B's primary demonstrated limitation is held-out generalization under sparse token-type coverage and a short public fit. The training arms fit their small data, yet no selected decoder produced an exact panel record or surpassed historical A1. The MLP result does not show that nonlinear capacity is useless; it shows no benefit under this data and stopping procedure. Historical-A1 provenance prevents a strong training-efficiency claim.

## Next experiment and change-direction criterion

Track B deserves the next experiment, with a controlled comparison before broadening the data. Train the historical standalone InputLens recipe and the three Track B variants on the same public fitting records, observations, labels, validation split, normalization, initialization, optimizer budget, and stopping rule, then run a separate broader-coverage condition spanning both input styles and token types. This resolves whether the current gap is caused mainly by fitting procedure/objective or by public-data coverage. If the matched-data comparison leaves the B arms near the current ceiling, capacity or objective becomes the leading hypothesis; if broader coverage then lifts held-out accuracy toward historical A1 while preserving no-A2 inference, that supports a larger fit and canonical dual-benchmark confirmation. A flat result on both controls should shift effort toward a structured/contextual decoder or another constrained inverse.

Track A merits only one bounded solver follow-up: test a line-search, contraction safeguard, or Jacobian-controlled update on disjoint matched public controls, with a fixed evaluation budget. Require both monotone cycle-residual reduction and token-accuracy improvement. Stable matched convergence and a discrete recovery gain would justify retesting the shifted target. Continued branch oscillation, residual above one, or unchanged near-zero late-ladder accuracy should end fixed-point refinement for this model family.
