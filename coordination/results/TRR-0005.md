## Optimization, generalization, and limits

The retained TRR4 affine diagnostic, run before joint fitting, reached 99.9936% token accuracy on original-like data (1,192/1,200 exact records) but 90.4447% on enriched data (3/1,200 exact records). All eight final fits reached 100% on their fitting streams under the declared exposure. These are optimization and fit-stream checks; the fresh table is the generalization evidence. The initial attention diagnostic found BOS saturation in the old dot-product causal branch. The fixed-QK-scale-4 qknorm repair was completed before fresh inference and improved the tested public-validation routing. This supports the repaired registered branch and does not justify rejecting attention capacity broadly.

The enrichment intervention changes public token coverage, natural context, domain mixture, and a modest controlled supplement together. It therefore demonstrates a coverage/context intervention rather than isolating vocabulary frequency. The fitting bank contains 1,200 records and 124,371 post-BOS positions per arm; each arm uses 3,000 steps × 512 post-BOS draws, or 1.536 million draws. The enriched bank is 600 Alpaca + 300 Pile + 180 Finance + 120 controlled records (60 each), changing distinct post-BOS IDs from 11,265 to 15,602 (7,630 new and 3,293 lost) and scanning 11.474 million additional public occurrences during preparation. Both target conditions use actual prefix forwards as paired observations: positionwise arms use same-position `H_i`, causal arms use `H_{≤i}`, and no A2 fallback is used. `H_i` already contains its native context; the causal comparison tests adding earlier hidden states beyond that same-position representation. Natural source selection is disjoint from the fresh holdout, but exact outcomes remain sparse. The target shift is source-paired and descriptive. The 128-token fresh records are evaluated through padded 192-token capture for future-pad invariance, while fitting used 8×192. These geometry and data differences are documented rather than silently treated as equivalent.
