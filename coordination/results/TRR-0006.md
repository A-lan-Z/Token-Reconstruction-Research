# TRR-0006 final handoff — frozen pair

TRR-0006 evaluated the two frozen enriched methods on 3,072 paired natural sources (1,536 per domain) in four domain/target cells. The public panel, H128 observations, eight prediction artifacts, public freeze, and one truth-scoring pass completed under the frozen plan. The registered result is **`positionwise_default`**: the incremental causal advantage is excluded at the +0.5 pp token and +5 pp exact margins in every cell. There is no evidence of material harm, and the cost qualification passes separately.

H_i already contains context. This comparison tests the incremental value of earlier observed activations. The largest practical upper bounds are +0.174 pp token and +3.285 pp exact recovery; absolute exact recovery remains only 2.2–2.9% on Pile and 22.8–26.4% on Finance. These are complete 127-token post-BOS clips, not complete original documents.

- Geometry is fixed at full public forward batches of 8 × 192, retaining H128 including BOS, scoring all 127 post-BOS positions, and one-record decoder calls in chunks of 8. No new fit, architecture, teacher, compression, or plain-affine substitution occurred.
- The only sample revision was the one-time frozen increase from 1,024 to 1,536 per domain. The conditional IID planning rationale and limits are in the decision plan; it is not a guarantee on this natural panel.

## Final decision and next question

**`positionwise_default`** is the final decision. The causal context advantage was excluded at the registered practical margins in all four declared cells, with no material-harm evidence and qualified cost. This is limited to the two frozen enriched states, the natural 1,536-per-domain panel, the two target conditions, and 128-token clips; it does not claim universal diagonal superiority.

The next architecture experiment should keep this pair frozen and test a preregistered additional natural domain and target-update trajectory. It would resolve whether the positionwise default transfers beyond these two domains and this single synthetic weight shift. We would reconsider the default if the causal decoder supports a registered practical benefit while meeting harm and cost limits on that independent setting. Do not automatically double this panel again. Improving absolute standalone recovery toward the historical A1+A2 quality level remains a separate priority; P04 owns its teacher-supervision work.
