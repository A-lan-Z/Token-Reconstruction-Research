# TRR-0003 — Initial probes for simpler token reconstruction

## Research objective

We are restarting this research to investigate **simpler reconstruction mechanisms**, not merely better configurations of historical A1+A2.

Pursue two complementary goals:

**Track A — No offline fitting.** Reconstruct using permitted public resources and observed activations, without a trained inverse/lens, fitted calibration, or auxiliary-data training phase. This is the owner’s original meaning of “simpler and more elegant.”

**Track B — No A2 at inference.** One-off training on permitted public data is acceptable if the deployed method is a compact decoder that reconstructs accurately without candidate-by-candidate public-prefix simulation. This could substantially reduce reconstruction overhead during online fine-tuning.

A1+A2 remains a comparator, not the architecture new methods must retain. A method that removes fitting but requires expensive online optimization must have that cost reported honestly; likewise, a trained decoder must include its preparation cost and retained model footprint.

**This round should produce informative pilot results, not a final replacement claim.** You have discretion over architectures, optimizers, datasets, and implementation details. Adjust the suggested experiments when a better diagnostic becomes apparent, and explain the change.

## 2. Track A: test a genuine checkpoint-only reconstruction mechanism

The initial question is:

> Can public model structure provide a substantially better inverse than directly comparing boundary activations with input embeddings, without training a lens?

Implement one credible structural or numerical approach. A reasonable starting point is continuous embedding inversion or residual/blockwise inversion using the public prefix, but choose a different approach if your analysis supports it.

Do not initialize this arm with a fitted A1 lens or quietly introduce data-fitted parameters. Distinguish fixed algorithm settings from fitted resources, and disclose development choices.

First test whether the mechanism behaves correctly on observations generated by the matched public model. Then, on identical inputs, test a target/surrogate mismatch with target weights unavailable to reconstruction. Keep the matched case labelled as a diagnostic control, not evidence of unknown-target recovery.

We need to distinguish:

- Failure to solve even the matched inverse.
- Failure when the target differs from the public surrogate.
- Successful inversion that is too expensive to be useful.

For iterative approaches, inspect the relationship between activation residual, token correctness, and iteration cost. A smaller residual is not sufficient evidence of better token recovery.

## 3. Track B: give standalone learned decoding a fair initial test

The initial question is:

> Was the earlier direct inverse weak because of its fitting procedure or restricted model class, rather than because A2 search is essential?

Build a small, interpretable comparison around direct token prediction. Suggested ingredients are an affine decoder trained with token-classification loss, the existing angular-loss inverse, and one compact nonlinear or causal-contextual decoder. Choose the smallest set that distinguishes useful hypotheses.

Use comparable public fitting data and report differences in training effort. Check whether models can fit a small training subset, inspect learning curves, and separate fitting failure from held-out generalization failure. Do not interpret one arbitrary short training schedule as a decisive rejection of the entire direction.

**The evaluated standalone decoder must emit tokens without A2 fallback.** Top-K recall is a diagnostic, not its success metric.

Public tokens provide legitimate training supervision. Teacher assistance or distillation is optional, not required for this round; prioritize determining whether direct supervision already yields a promising search-free decoder.

## 4. Experimental discipline without an unnecessary sweep

Prefer the existing model family and cut-4 setting initially, so mechanism differences remain interpretable. Use modest public development panels spanning more than one input style. Pair identical records across matched and shifted targets where possible.

Keep fitting/development data separate from canonical evaluations and future confirmation data. Never use current-record truth to make reconstruction decisions. Preserve the charter’s causal-state rules, and clearly label any teacher-prefix diagnostic rather than reporting it as deployed reconstruction.

Treat these variants as exploratory probes. Do not promote one as a replacement without the required benchmark comparisons and independent confirmation. Avoid launching the full historical configuration matrix merely to screen an idea.

Choose sensible local compute limits after preflight, preserve failed attempts, and stop or scale down on resource anomalies. Do not incur paid compute charges.
