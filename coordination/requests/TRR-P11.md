# Recovery amendment — Rebuild the fixed-readout pair as a new replication

## Decision

The earlier prohibition on retraining is superseded for this narrowly
defined recovery study.

Both exact PR20 selected fixed-readout checkpoints are unavailable after
the documented bounded recovery search. Preserve the original TRR-0011
frozen-checkpoint confirmation as blocked/incomplete due to missing
artifacts. Do not record a scientific failure for an evaluation that
never ran.

Proceed with an explicitly named successor run or task:
a new-weight replication of the documented B0/B1 fixed-readout experiment.

Rebuilt checkpoints are new models unless exact original tensor identity
is independently verified. Matching filenames, seeds, configurations,
old scores, or a limited set of predictions is not sufficient.

## Scope

Rebuild only:
- current-bank B0 with fixed public readout;
- expanded-bank B1 with fixed public readout.

Do not rerun directional-readout arms, change the architecture or loss,
expand the data further, or start a new teacher/context/solver experiment.

Do not repeat fits until the old headline accuracy is recovered.

## Agent 1 — reconstruction and durable model package

First inventory the required surviving inputs:

- the common starting decoder and its exact state identity;
- public model, tokenizer, and normalized readout resources;
- ordered B0/B1 bank definitions and preprocessing;
- activation-generation geometry and numerical settings;
- training schedule, checkpoint grid, and validation-selection rule.

Regenerate reproducible preparation assets from documented public sources
where necessary. Record all deviations from the original execution.

If a prerequisite starting model or bank cannot be reproduced, state the
specific missing dependency. Do not silently substitute another ancestor
and describe the run as the same recipe.

Use the documented common fitting budget and checkpoint-selection rule.
The rebuilt selected steps need not equal the old winning steps.
Treat all selection examples strictly as development data.

Before fitting, record a bounded local resource plan. No paid compute
is authorized. Reuse sound code and tests rather than rebuilding the
research platform.

Save checkpoints to persistent storage during training, not solely to
temporary directories. Preserve both selected models and the exact
deployment resources/configuration they require.

## Agent 2 — exclusions and independent evaluation

Complete the unresolved source-exclusion audit while reconstruction proceeds.

Explicitly include gradient-fitting, inherited fitting, checkpoint-selection,
calibration, and previously opened evaluation sources in the appropriate
exclusion ledgers. Check both source identities and canonical sequences.

All PR20 final sources are development/selection-overlapping material.
They must not be reused as an independent final test.

Freeze the new evaluation plan before final outcomes are opened.
Keep the reconstructed methods identical across the compared sources.
Use source-paired target conditions and report domains separately.

A common A1+A2 subset may remain in the plan, with its denominator explicit.
No full historical method sweep is required.

## Mandatory restore gate

Before scientific evaluation:

1. Store the actual selected weight files and required deployment package
   in durable storage.
2. Create a second verified copy outside the same reset/failure boundary.
3. From a clean working directory, Agent 2 must retrieve and load the
   package without relying on the training worktree or temporary paths.
4. Verify file/tensor identities and reproduce a fixed, already-opened
   public smoke test.

A manifest, hash, pointer, or successful earlier replay is not a substitute
for retrievable checkpoint bytes.

Do not declare the package complete until this restore gate passes.

## Scientific interpretation

The new study tests a rebuilt pair and the documented fitting procedure.
It does not complete the original exact-checkpoint confirmation or
retroactively establish PR20's independence.

Preserve PR20's scores and label their selection overlap. Report whether
the new pair's expanded-data advantage generalizes to unused sources.

Distinguish a reproduced gain, a smaller effect, an actual regression,
and imprecise evidence. Do not automatically attribute a difference from
PR20 entirely to selection bias or entirely to training variation.

One new paired fit is one replication, not proof of seed-independent
training reliability.

## Preservation and handoff

Keep original task records and artifact-loss evidence unchanged.
Use new run/model identities and task-local status. No PR merges or
global-registry changes are authorized.

P03's sealed holdout remains out of scope. Continue approved hash-only
coordination and preserve evaluator/reconstructor separation.

Report:
- what survived and what was rebuilt;
- exact new model and preparation identities;
- deviations, fitting costs, and failures;
- durable storage locations and verified restore evidence;
- the genuinely independent evaluation results.

The parallel transfer study may resume only after the new model package
is frozen and recoverable, under its separate approved plan.
