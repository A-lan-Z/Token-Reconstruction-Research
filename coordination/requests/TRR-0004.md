## What the pilot does and does not show

The new decoders fit small training subsets but generalize poorly. Only 510/936 validation token occurrences had IDs represented in their fitting labels. Historical A1's documented recipe uses roughly 125,571 fitting positions, versus 4,992 here; exact provenance for the retained A1 asset remains incomplete. Different corpora, context lengths, schedules, and parameterizations also confound the comparison.

Two source-level differences deserve specific tests:

- The new CE models add a trainable per-vocabulary bias and fix the logit scale at 16. Historical A1 has an affine hidden-space bias, a learned global scale, and no per-vocabulary bias. A never-observed label receives only suppressive cross-entropy gradients on its vocabulary bias. This is a possible generalization problem, not a demonstrated cause of the accuracy gap.
- The MLP replaces the general affine correction with `h + U GELU(D h)`, with a 256-wide bottleneck. Its pre-normalization correction varies in at most a 256-dimensional subspace, apart from a constant bias. It is not a strict extension of the full affine inverse. Its failure does not reject nonlinear decoding in general.

Check that the retained historical lens can be expressed in the standalone inference path without training: map its affine parameters faithfully, remove vocabulary bias, and match scale, normalization, and numerical execution. Compare token ranks/predictions and logits where meaningful. This tests the implementation/representation bridge; it is not a new fitting result.

Disabling bias after training is not equivalent to training without it, so follow promising evidence with a controlled fit.

Use these checks to eliminate explanations before launching more training. Do not turn them into a large hyperparameter sweep.

## 3. Use the result to test a substantive improvement, not just recover A1

If fitting differences explain the gap, do not stop at reproducing 92–96% A1 performance. Use the remaining bounded experimental budget for one compact improvement over the competent affine baseline.

A preferred test is a causal activation-context decoder predicting `x_i` from `H_0...H_i`, without guessed-token inputs or A2 fallback. Preserve the competent positionwise path and initialize any added contextual correction so that the model initially reproduces it, where practical. This avoids asking a randomly initialized contextual model to relearn everything before testing context. Compare with the unchanged positionwise baseline and a parameter-matched positionwise nonlinear alternative, or another equally informative control, so that a gain can be attributed to context rather than merely additional parameters.

A positionwise nonlinear extension is also reasonable when pilot diagnostics favour it, but retain a full affine path instead of inadvertently restricting that path to the original 256-dimensional correction. You may choose a better design and explain why. If the fitting baseline still cannot be established, return the discriminating evidence rather than performing indefinite retraining or making an architecture-ceiling claim.
